Principal's Message

Leadership, learning and holistic development of our students

Message from the Principal

Dr. (Mrs.) Anu Kapil

Principal

I welcome you all to the S.D.S.P.M. College for Women, Rayya with pride. The college stands for excellence and continuously strives to maintain high standards of education and overall development of its students.

The college is committed to providing quality education by equipping students with knowledge, skills, confidence and a positive approach towards life. Along with academic excellence, students are encouraged to participate in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities which contribute to the development of a versatile personality.

We believe that a friendly and inspiring academic environment nurtures creativity, passion, resilience and leadership qualities. Our teachers remain committed to guiding students and facilitating their academic as well as personal growth.

The management takes keen interest in initiatives aimed at the overall development of students and faculty. The dedicated teaching and non-teaching staff work together with commitment towards the mission and goals of the institution.

Our students continue to participate in academic, sports and cultural activities while maintaining high moral values and ethics. We believe in creating opportunities that help students discover their potential and work confidently towards their goals.

“The Sky is the only limit.”

My good wishes to all.

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women, Rayya, Amritsar, Punjab.
