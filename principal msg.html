Principal's Message

Message from the Principal of the college.

Principal

Dr. (Mrs.) Anu Kapil

It is our endeavour to provide students with an educational environment that supports academic learning, personal growth and responsible citizenship.

Our students are encouraged to develop confidence, creativity, leadership qualities and a strong sense of responsibility.

The college aims to combine classroom learning with co-curricular activities and opportunities that contribute to the overall development of young women.

Students are encouraged to make meaningful use of the facilities, academic resources and activities available on campus and to prepare themselves for further education, careers and responsible participation in society.

We welcome our students to learn with dedication, participate with confidence and move forward with values.

Dr. (Mrs.) Anu Kapil

Principal
