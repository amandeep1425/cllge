About Our College

Education With Purpose

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women is located at Rayya, Amritsar, Punjab. The college is committed to providing quality education and creating opportunities for women to grow academically and personally.

With academic programmes, campus facilities and student activities, the college provides an environment where students can learn, develop confidence and prepare for their future.
