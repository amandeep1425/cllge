About Our College

Building Knowledge, Confidence & Opportunities

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women is a women's college located at Rayya in Amritsar district. Established in 1974, the institution is committed to providing higher education and opportunities for women.

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate education
  • Academic and career-oriented learning
  • Student development and co-curricular activities
  • Library, laboratories and campus facilities
