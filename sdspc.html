About Our College

Education With Purpose

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women is located at Rayya, District Amritsar, Punjab. The college is dedicated to providing opportunities for women to pursue higher education and develop academically and personally.

The college provides academic programmes together with student activities and campus facilities that support learning, confidence, skills and overall development.
