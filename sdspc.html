About Our College

Education With Purpose

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women is located at Rayya, Amritsar, Punjab. The college is committed to providing quality education and opportunities for women.

Academic programmes, learning resources, campus facilities and student activities provide opportunities for students to learn, develop confidence and prepare for their future.
