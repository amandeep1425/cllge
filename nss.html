About NSS

The National Service Scheme (NSS) provides students with opportunities to participate in community service and social development activities.

The department encourages students to develop a sense of social responsibility, leadership and teamwork through various activities and awareness programmes.

NSS Activities

  • Community Service Programmes
  • Social Awareness Campaigns
  • Cleanliness Drives
  • Health Awareness Activities
  • Community Development Programmes
