Student Service

National Service Scheme

Learning through service, awareness and community participation.

NSS Activities

NSS provides students opportunities to participate in community-oriented activities and develop a sense of social responsibility.

Major Areas

  • Seven-day special camps
  • Health and awareness programmes
  • AIDS awareness activities
  • Drug-awareness programmes
  • Tree plantation and environmental activities
  • Cleanliness and Swachhta activities
  • Community outreach
  • Youth-development activities

Learning Through Service

Participation in NSS helps students develop teamwork, leadership, social awareness and a responsible attitude towards the community.
