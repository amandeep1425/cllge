About The College

Our history, vision and commitment to women's education.

Since 1974

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women

Established in 1974, the college is situated at Rayya in Amritsar district of Punjab. The institution was established to provide higher education opportunities for women, particularly in the surrounding rural area.

The college is affiliated with Guru Nanak Dev University and offers undergraduate and postgraduate education along with career-oriented and diploma programmes.

  • Women's higher education
  • Value-based learning
  • Academic and personality development
  • Career and skill-oriented opportunities
