About Our College

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women

Education With Purpose

Shaheed Darshan Singh Pheruman Memorial College for Women is located at Rayya, Amritsar, Punjab.

The college focuses on providing quality education and creating opportunities for women to develop academically, personally and professionally.

Along with academic programmes, students can participate in different activities and use campus facilities that support their learning and overall development.

What We Offer

Academic Learning

A range of academic programmes and computer-related courses for students.

Student Activities

NSS, NCC, Red Ribbon Club and other activities support student participation.

Campus Facilities

Learning resources and campus facilities support students during their academic journey.
